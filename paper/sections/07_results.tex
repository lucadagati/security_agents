\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

We report the heuristic adaptation ladder (H1, H2, H5; RQ0--RQ3, RQ5) and
the language-model campaign (H4, H7) under the protocol of
Section~\ref{sec:method}.

\subsection{Heuristic adaptation ladder}
\label{sec:results-ladder}
Table~\ref{tab:ladder} and Fig.~\ref{fig:ladder} summarise the factorial
over six seeds with forty episodes each. Static attackers are detected in
$98.3{\pm}1.0\%$ of episodes with low ASR ($0.029$--$0.046$) regardless of
defender adaptation. When the attacker adapts, mean DR falls to
$0.558{\pm}0.047$ and ASR rises modestly to $0.067$--$0.071$; an adaptive
defender does not further reduce DR against a static attacker (H1 supported
on detection more than on success).

The dominant mechanism is stealth pacing: adaptive attackers insert
low-noise pauses after noisy exploits so suspicion decays before the next
probe, yielding emergence tags \emph{delayed attack} and \emph{strategic
patience} (H5) despite objectives that mention only exfiltration and cost.

\input{figures/ladder_bars}

Persistent matchups are classified as arms races: late-episode signature
change stays elevated (Fig.~\ref{fig:arms}). On a representative run, rolling
DR falls while attacker stealth rises from $0.5$ to $0.9$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:episode-dynamics}), consistent with H2. CEP peaks when both
sides adapt ($0.454{\pm}0.021$ to $0.465{\pm}0.043$).

\subsection{Language-model evaluation}
\label{sec:results-llm}
Pure LLM policies frequently fail to complete the offensive chain; we
therefore report hybrid B3--B5 cells (Section~\ref{sec:method-policies}).

Table~\ref{tab:llm} and Fig.~\ref{fig:hybrid-asr} summarise six seeds with
thirty episodes each. Only episodic hybrid B4 reaches clearly positive mean
ASR ($0.139{\pm}0.052$) with non-trivial CEP ($0.247{\pm}0.021$). Static
hybrid B3 acts but never exfiltrates ($\mathrm{ASR}=0$,
$\mathrm{DR}=0.939{\pm}0.020$); persistent B5 stays near the floor
($\mathrm{ASR}=0.022{\pm}0.014$). H4 is not supported: longer memory does
not beat episodic memory.

\input{figures/hybrid_memory_bars}

Supplementary conditions (Table~\ref{tab:llm_ablation}) show non-monotonic
model scale (H7), high ASR ($\approx0.35$) when a heuristic attacker faces a
hybrid defender, and near-zero ASR in the reverse matchup, indicating that
chain completion dominates nominal capability. Extended horizons and a small
$3{\times}3$ cell do not replicate the ladder's co-evolutionary effects.
